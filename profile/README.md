## 🚀 Overview
FxFactory Pro for Windows brings a large library of visual-effects plugins into the video editors you already use. Instead of switching tools, you install the plugins through the free FxFactory app and then reach for them from inside a host editor such as Adobe Premiere Pro or After Effects, where they show up alongside your other effects. It solves the everyday problem of building polished looks, transitions, and titles without leaving your timeline, so your creative work and your editing stay in one place.

## ✨ Features
- **Effects inside your host editor** — Apply generators, filters, and looks without exporting to a separate program.
- **Transitions library** — Move between clips with animated wipes, dissolves, and stylized cuts.
- **Titles and lower thirds** — Drop in animated text presets and adjust them to match your project.
- **Managed by the free FxFactory app** — Browse, install, and update plugins from a single hub that handles both free and paid titles.
- **Non-destructive controls** — Tweak parameters directly on the clip and re-render as your edit evolves.

## 📥 Installation
1. Click the **Download** button above to get the free FxFactory app for Windows.
2. Run the downloaded installer and follow the on-screen setup steps.
3. Open the FxFactory app to browse and install plugins; many are free, while FxFactory Pro is the paid tier that unlocks premium effects.
4. Open your host editor, such as Adobe Premiere Pro, where the installed FxFactory Pro plugins appear in your effects library.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Launch a host editor such as Adobe Premiere Pro or After Effects and open a project.
2. Find the FxFactory Pro effects, transitions, or titles in your editor's effects list.
3. Drag one onto a clip or between two clips in your timeline.
4. Adjust the parameters in your editor's controls until the look matches your vision.
5. Preview and render your sequence as usual.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is FxFactory Pro free?**
The FxFactory app that installs and manages the plugins is a free download and includes many free plugins. FxFactory Pro is the paid tier that unlocks premium effects; you buy only the packages you want.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
FxFactory Pro runs on current 64-bit versions of Windows, including Windows 10 and Windows 11, provided your host video editor is also installed and supported.

**Is my data secure?**
The plugins run locally inside your host editor and process your footage on your own machine, so your projects stay under your control.
